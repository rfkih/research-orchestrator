The backtest covers SOLUSDT spot data sourced from Binance on the four-hour bar from 2024-01-01 through 2026-05-26, a window of 876 calendar days encompassing approximately 5,256 four-hour bars. The initial capital is set to 1,000 USDT. Transaction costs are modelled as 0.075% per side, consistent with the Binance standard-tier SPOT maker/taker blended rate, applied symmetrically to all fills. No additional slippage model is applied beyond this fee assumption, meaning the reported metrics constitute an upper bound on achievable live performance. All P&L figures reported in subsequent sections are expressed in raw USDT on the 1,000 USDT capital base.
