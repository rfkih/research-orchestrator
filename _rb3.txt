The quarterly performance pattern directly contradicts the stationarity assumption that any robust trading strategy must satisfy. A system whose entire positive P&L derives from two quarters in 2024, specifically 2024Q1 at 6.418 USDT and 2024Q3 at 5.021 USDT, and then produces zero winning trades across every subsequent quarter with recorded trades cannot be considered statistically stable. The degradation is not gradual; it is abrupt and total. From 2024Q4 onward, no trade closes in profit across six quarters, suggesting the mechanism exploited a specific volatility or mean-reversion regime present in the early 2024 Solana market that has not recurred. This pattern is consistent with a strategy that over-adapted to a narrow historical window rather than one detecting a durable structural inefficiency.
