Two specific modifications address the identified failure mode directly and merit evaluation in subsequent research. Relaxing the RSI oversold threshold from 25 to 30 or 35 would materially increase signal frequency by capturing a broader set of oversold conditions; this increases the risk of entering deteriorating trends but resolves the sample-size barrier that prevents any statistical inference from being drawn. Alternatively, applying the same oscillator-only mechanism on the one-hour bar rather than the four-hour bar would multiply available bars by a factor of four over the same calendar window, generating a substantially larger trade sample without altering the entry logic. Either path must ultimately produce a trade count sufficient to compute DSR and establish a profit-factor confidence interval whose lower bound exceeds 1.0; until that threshold is met, the MRO concept on SOLUSDT earns no further evaluation resources.
