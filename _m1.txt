The MRO strategy generates long-only entry signals when the closing price of a four-hour bar simultaneously satisfies two conditions: the close touches or breaches the lower Bollinger Band, computed over a 20-period lookback at 2.0 standard deviations, and the RSI reads at or below the oversold threshold of 25. This intersection deliberately restricts entries to episodes of coincident price-band extremity and momentum exhaustion, creating a filter that favours deeply oversold bounces rather than graduated pullbacks. Position exits are governed by a time-based ceiling: any trade that remains open beyond 48 four-hour bars, eight calendar days, is closed at market regardless of profit or loss, preventing indefinite capital lock-up in stalled recoveries.
