The parameter space is defined by four axes: the Bollinger Band lookback period (bbPeriod), the Bollinger Band standard-deviation multiplier (bbStdMult), the maximum number of four-hour bars a position may be held before forced exit (maxBarsHeld), and the RSI oversold ceiling (rsiOversoldMax). A full Cartesian grid sweep over these four axes yields 24 candidate parameter combinations. The best-performing combination identified by the sweep is bbPeriod = 20, bbStdMult = 2.0, maxBarsHeld = 48 bars equivalent to eight calendar days, and rsiOversoldMax = 25. All performance metrics reported in subsequent sections refer to this best configuration unless otherwise explicitly noted. The sweep produces no walk-forward results, as the trade volume is insufficient to partition into meaningful in-sample and out-of-sample folds.
