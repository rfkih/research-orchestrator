The study conducts a 24-cell Cartesian grid sweep over four strategy parameters, subjecting every candidate configuration to the V11 statistical gate, which requires a minimum completed trade count, a profit-factor 95% confidence interval whose lower bound exceeds 1.0, and a Deflated Sharpe Ratio at or above 0.95, and the V60 economic gate, which independently mandates an annualized CAGR of at least 10.0% computed on a 365-day geometric basis. The sweep additionally records hypothesis audit rows before each backtest submission, ensuring that the cumulative trial count correctly inflates DSR deflation to penalise selection bias across all 24 cells.
