Statistical significance is assessed under the V11 gate, which requires three conditions to hold simultaneously: the number of completed trades must reach a minimum threshold sufficient for valid inference; the lower bound of the profit-factor 95% bootstrap confidence interval must exceed 1.0, ensuring the observed edge cannot plausibly be zero or negative at standard confidence; and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio must reach or exceed 0.95, where DSR deflates the observed Sharpe by the number of cumulative historical trials to account for selection bias across the sweep. The V60 economic gate operates independently and requires the annualized geometric return, computed on a strict 365-day year, to reach or exceed 10.0%. Both gate sets must pass for a configuration to earn a SIGNIFICANT_EDGE or PARKED verdict; failure on any single criterion automatically yields INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
