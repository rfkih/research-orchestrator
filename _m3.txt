Regime attribution is performed post hoc by applying a rolling 200-period EMA to the same four-hour price series and classifying each bar as BULL when the EMA slope is positive and BEAR when negative. Trade outcomes are then partitioned by the regime label active at entry. Quarterly P&L breakdowns complement the regime view by exposing temporal clustering of performance that aggregate statistics would otherwise obscure. All regime and quarterly figures represent raw USDT P&L and the fraction of trades within each stratum that closed at a positive profit.
